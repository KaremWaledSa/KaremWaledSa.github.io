PROJECT CONTEXT

Mobile prototype · Pose analysis

01 / ENGINEERING PROBLEM

What the system needed to do.

Translate a live camera stream into exercise-specific motion feedback while keeping the analysis local to the device.

02 / ENGINEERING APPROACH

How the work comes together.

Camera frames feed Google ML Kit pose detection; body landmarks are converted into joint-angle measurements.

Exercise-specific thresholds, movement states, and cooldowns drive repetition counting and feedback.

The app combines Arabic/English exercise metadata, voice cues, workout state, and settings. The repository includes tests for pose services and application configuration.

03 / RESULT & SCOPE

What the work demonstrates.

A working mobile prototype and public source code. It is an educational fitness tool, not a clinically validated assessment system.
