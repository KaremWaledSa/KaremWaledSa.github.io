PROJECT CONTEXT

Team graduation project · 2022

01 / ENGINEERING PROBLEM

What the system needed to do.

Develop an existing open-source humanoid into an integrated assistive platform. The engineering work spans joint actuation, wiring, robot description, perception, and mobile navigation.

02 / ENGINEERING APPROACH

How the work comes together.

Recovered Arduino sketches cover joint motion, fingers, shoulders, potentiometer mapping, and calibration. These show the hardware-facing side of the project.

The team report documents encoder odometry and IMU fusion, Kinect-based mapping, and the ROS 1 move_base navigation stack with global and local costmaps.

The documented manipulation workflow goes from a SolidWorks assembly to URDF, MoveIt configuration, and a rosserial interface sending joint commands to Arduino.

03 / RESULT & SCOPE

What the work demonstrates.

A documented team robot-development project with firmware, CAD, and a final report. The galleries below show the team's ROS system and hardware work; they do not assign every thesis subsystem to one contributor.
