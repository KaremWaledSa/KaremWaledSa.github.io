PROJECT CONTEXT

Professional software · Anonymized case study

01 / ENGINEERING PROBLEM

What the system needed to do.

Connect service information and daily operational work into consistent, reviewable ERP workflows.

02 / ENGINEERING APPROACH

How the work comes together.

Developed the customer-service workspace and related follow-up records.

Implemented delivery and daily-work flows with relational models, views, and structured validation.

03 / RESULT & SCOPE

What the work demonstrates.

An anonymized architecture and contribution case study. Business names, identifiable screenshots, and production records are excluded.

04 / KAREM WALED — RESPONSIBILITY

What I owned and contributed.

  • Translated service and delivery requirements into Odoo data models and workflows.
  • Implemented Python business logic, XML views, relational fields, permissions, and validation.
  • Developed customer-service and delivery/daily-work flows while protecting identifiable business data.
  • Handled debugging and integration across model, view, and workflow layers.

05 / SYSTEM BREAKDOWN

How the system is structured.

  • Customer 360 workspace links service context and follow-up records.
  • Relational models preserve the connection between customers, tasks, status, and operational history.
  • Delivery and daily-work states structure how records move through review and completion.
  • Access rules and validation constrain who can perform sensitive actions and what constitutes a valid record.

06 / TECHNICAL IMPLEMENTATION

Engineering decisions and execution.

01

Data model

Odoo ORM models encode business entities, their relationships, status fields, and derived behavior.

02

Operator experience

XML forms, lists, actions, and menus organize the workflow around the information needed at each step.

03

Workflow integrity

Validation and controlled state transitions help keep service and delivery records consistent and traceable.
